Si të kërkosh fshirjen

Llogaria e Tokërrgjikut është vetëm një emër që e shkruan vetë, bashkë me pikët e lojës. Nuk ka email dhe nuk ka fjalëkalim, ndaj fshirja nuk kërkon as verifikim.

1. Nga vetë aplikacioni (mënyra e rekomanduar)
  1. Hap aplikacionin Tokërrgjiku.
  2. Prek Luaj online.
  3. Prek ikonën ⚙ Cilësimet e llogarisë lart djathtas.
  4. Prek Fshi llogarinë dhe konfirmo.
Llogaria fshihet në atë çast. Aplikacioni vazhdon të punojë — loja kundër telefonit dhe loja në të njëjtën pajisje nuk kërkojnë llogari fare.
2. Me email, nëse nuk e ke më aplikacionin

Shkruaj te admin@spacecode.tech me lëndën «Fshirje llogarie — Tokërrgjiku» dhe me emrin e lojtarit që përdor në lojë. Kërkesa kryhet brenda 30 ditësh.

Delete your account and data

Tokërrgjiku (Nine Men's Morris), developed by Shaban Ejupi · the site tokerrgjik.shabanejupi.tech and the Android app com.ejupishaban.tokerrgjik · updated 30 July 2026

How to request deletion

1. In the app (recommended)
  1. Open Tokërrgjiku.
  2. Tap Play online.
  3. Tap the ⚙ Account settings icon at the top right.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.
Deletion is immediate. The app keeps working: playing the phone and pass-and-play need no account at all.
2. By email, if you no longer have the app — write to admin@spacecode.tech, subject "Account deletion — Tokërrgjiku", including the player name you use in the game. Requests are handled within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhat happens
The player name you choseDeleted immediately
Points (Elo), wins, losses and drawsDeleted immediately
Sign-in tokenDeleted immediately from the server and from the phone
A match in progressClosed as a resignation, so your opponent is not left waiting for a move that will never come
Past matchesKept, but with no link to you: your name becomes "Deleted player". A match has two sides, and removing it would rewrite your opponent's history and rating.
Server technical logs (IP address, request time)Kept up to 30 days for security, then dropped automatically. They are not tied to your name.
Advertising IDNever stored by us. Used by Google AdMob to serve ads. Clear or reset it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
Scores and saved game inside the phoneNever sent to the server. Removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

There is no additional retention period: after deletion the server holds no record that identifies you.
