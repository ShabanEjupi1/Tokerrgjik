Shkurt

Tokërrgjik nuk kërkon llogari, nuk kërkon email dhe nuk kërkon numër telefoni. Loja kundër kompjuterit ose me një shok në të njëjtin telefon luhet pa internet.

Aplikacioni në Android shfaq reklama nga Google AdMob; faqja në internet nuk shfaq reklama. Shih «Reklamat» më poshtë.

Çfarë ruhet në telefonin tënd

  • emri që zgjedh vetë;
  • niveli i fundit i kompjuterit dhe nëse i do tingujt;
  • numri i fitoreve, humbjeve dhe barazimeve kundër kompjuterit;
  • një lojë e papërfunduar, që ta vazhdosh më vonë;
  • një kod i rastësishëm identifikimi, nëse luan online.

Të gjitha këto rrinë vetëm në pajisje dhe fshihen kur çinstalon aplikacionin.

Çfarë dërgohet kur luan online

Vetëm atëherë, dhe vetëm te serveri ynë (tokerrgjik.shabanejupi.tech):

  • emri që ke zgjedhur;

Serveri ynë nuk merr vendndodhjen, kontaktet, fotot apo identifikuesin e reklamave.

Reklamat

Aplikacioni në Android shfaq reklama përmes Google AdMob. Për të shfaqur një reklamë, SDK-ja e Google-it lexon nga pajisja identifikuesin e reklamave (Advertising ID), adresën IP dhe të dhëna të përgjithshme të pajisjes, dhe i përpunon sipas politikës së vet. Ato të dhëna shkojnë te Google, nuk kalojnë nga serveri i Tokërrgjikut dhe nuk lidhen me emrin, pikët ose ndeshjet e tua.

  • Ku shfaqen: te menyja dhe pas një loje të mbaruar — jo pas çdo loje, dhe kurrë mbi tabelën gjatë radhës tënde. Reklamat me shpërblim shfaqen vetëm nëse i zgjedh vetë, për një këshillë kundër kompjuterit.
  • Pëlqimi: në Bashkimin Evropian dhe në MB shfaqet formulari i pëlqimit i Google-it (UMP) para reklamës së parë.
  • Filtrimi: reklamat kufizohen te klasifikimi «G», dhe kategoritë e bixhozit, alkoolit, takimeve dhe kredive me kamatë janë bllokuar nga llogaria e AdMob-it.
  • Kush e mban: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Identifikuesin e reklamave mund ta zerosh ose ta fshish kurdo nga Cilësimet → Google → Reklamat, në vetë Android-in.

Privacy Policy

Tokërrgjik · updated 30 July 2026

In short

Tokërrgjik has no accounts and asks for no email or phone number. Playing against the computer, or against a friend on the same phone, works with no internet at all.

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob; the website shows no ads. See “Ads” below.

Stored on your device

  • the display name you choose;
  • your last difficulty setting and whether sounds are on;
  • your win / loss / draw counts against the computer;
  • one unfinished game, so you can resume it;
  • a random identifier, only if you play online.

All of it stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Sent when you play online

Only then, and only to our own server (tokerrgjik.shabanejupi.tech):

  • the display name you chose;
  • the random identifier (it is not tied to an account, an email address or any device identifier);
  • the moves of the match and its result.

Our server receives no location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.

Ads

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve an ad, Google’s SDK reads your advertising ID, IP address and general device information from the device and processes them under its own policy. That data goes to Google, does not pass through the Tokërrgjik server, and is not linked to your name, rating or matches.

  • Where they appear: in the menu and after a finished game — not after every game, and never over the board on your turn. Rewarded ads are shown only if you choose one, in exchange for a hint against the computer.
  • Consent: in the EU and UK, Google’s consent form (UMP) is shown before the first ad.
  • Filtering: ads are limited to the “G” content rating, and gambling, alcohol, dating and interest-bearing credit categories are blocked at the AdMob account level.
  • Who holds it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android itself: Settings → Google → Ads.

Who sees it

Game data is never sold or handed to anyone. The only third party is Google AdMob, for the ads alone and only as described above. Beyond that the app contains no analytics library and no tracking of any kind.

Retention

Your name and rating are kept while you play. Finished matches are kept for the most recent 400 and then deleted automatically.

Account and data deletion

In the app: Play online → ⚙ Account settings → Delete account. It is immediate; no verification is needed, because there is no email and no password.

Full instructions, and a route without the app: tokerrgjik.shabanejupi.tech/fshi-te-dhenat.html — including the table of what is deleted and what is kept.

Or by email to admin@spacecode.tech with the name you use; requests are handled within 30 days.

Your name, rating and token are deleted immediately; a match in progress is closed as a resignation so your opponent is not left waiting. Earlier matches remain, but your name is replaced by “Deleted player”. For on-device data, uninstalling is enough.

Children

The game’s content is suitable for all ages, but the app is not directed at children and its Google Play target audience is 13 and over. The reason is the ads: an app declared for children falls under different advertising rules. No personal data is knowingly collected from children.
